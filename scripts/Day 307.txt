What Should I Wear?
It’s the middle of October. That means that it is autumn now, right? That depends on the day. The high temperature last Thursday was 30 degrees. That’s definitely summer weather! Then, it dropped down to 23 degrees last Sunday. It was a wonderful autumn day, and on Monday morning, the temperature was only 13.5 degrees. Now, it’s back up to 26 degrees during the day. I have to ask myself, “What should I wear?” I hear this question everywhere: from my family, from my friends, and especially from the weather reporter on the TV news. When I’m not sure, I wear a short-sleeved shirt, and I carry a jacket with me.
