I like Merucari, and I often use it. What I like most is that I can comfortably say goodbye to the items which once belonged to me. To put them online for sale, you have to clean them up as much as possible and take good photos so that they look attractive. It might sound like too much to do, but actually I need to do the work in order to say farewell to the items properly. While preparing them, a feeling of appreciation comes up inside me, and I want to say, “Thank you for having been with me. Be happy in your new place.” I also feel relieved that I don’t have to throw the items away. In fact, this is a kind of ceremony so that I can let things go without feeling guilty.
