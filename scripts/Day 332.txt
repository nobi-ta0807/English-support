A Great Time to Go Outside
It’s the last month of the year, but it’s still autumn here in Nagoya. The trees have finally started to change color. I often take my daughter’s dog for a walk, and we usually go to a park that has ginkgo trees. They are turning yellow, but there is still some green in the leaves. I look forward to walking down the street near my house that is lined with ginkgo trees. They drop their leaves and make a yellow carpet on the road. I like maple trees, too. They are turning red, and they are particularly beautiful when the sun hits them. Now is a great time to go outside!
