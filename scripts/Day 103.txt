If you like Ghibli movies, Ghibli Park is the place for you! You can find characters and places from Ghibli animated movies everywhere in the park. It is Nagoya’s newest theme park, and it’s growing. The park opened on November 1, 2022, with three areas: Ghibli’s Grand Warehouse, Hill of Youth, and Dondoko Forest. Then, yesterday, exactly one year later, they opened a new area, Mononoke Village. Unlike other theme parks, you don’t buy a ticket for the whole park. You buy one ticket for each area. Ghibli’s Grand Warehouse and Mononoke Village are included together in one ticket.
