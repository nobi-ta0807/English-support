A T-shirt without sleeves makes me comfortable. I made my own myself by cutting the sleeves off my worn-out T-shirt. I wore it in the summer even when men did not wear this kind of shirt in Japan. I had seen these shirts worn by American students in the United States, and I have been wearing a sleeveless shirt since then. When I am cycling, I wear it. It lets the wind come in and pass over the surface of my body, so I feel very comfortable when I am pedaling.
