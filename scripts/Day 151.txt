One day, when I woke up in the morning, one of my favorite flowers was gone! Who did such a terrible thing? Right after that, I witnessed the criminals committing the same crime again on the spot! They were birds! They must have woken up earlier than me to have breakfast secretly. I was so angry! But Mom, also a big fan of flowers, said calmly, “Let them do it. We’re all living together in this world.” I couldn’t understand how she could be so generous. Then, the next morning, I woke up to a loud shout, “Go away!” When I went downstairs, Mom was shouting madly, and ALL the flowers were gone! It looks like we cannot always live in harmony.
