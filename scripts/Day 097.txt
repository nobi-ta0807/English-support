What do you do with a 7-meter-long electric piano that lies on the ground? You step on it! The first time I saw this kind of piano was in the movie “Big,” a 1988 movie starring Tom Hanks. He sees a big piano in a toy shop, jumps on it, and starts to play a song. Then the owner of the store joins him. They play a couple of songs together. They are very good, but now there is an Italian group called “Pianoforte Gigante,” or Big Piano in English, that is really amazing. They don’t just play the big piano: they dance on the keys as gracefully as Irish dancers, and they play difficult songs perfectly. I don’t think that they have performed in Japan, but don’t worry. You can see their shows on the Internet.
