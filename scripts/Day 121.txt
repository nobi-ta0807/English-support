Now you can look around a library without leaving your house. The Nagoya City Library is celebrating its 100th anniversary and has just opened a virtual library. Anyone can go inside. I tried it on my computer, but it is easier to navigate on a smartphone. When you enter, you choose an avatar. Then your avatar walks or runs around. I went into the library and found a storytelling room. I sat down and listened to an old Japanese story about a magic pot that made rice porridge. I also tried reading “The Little Prince.” Nagoya hopes that younger people will come to this virtual library and get more interested in the real library.
