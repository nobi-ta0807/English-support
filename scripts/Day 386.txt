I went to Wales in the U.K. last summer for two weeks. I had a great time there. In particular, I remember an incident that happened in a pie shop near the beach. I bought a sausage pie for lunch there. When I tried to eat the pie, I dropped it on the road by mistake. I picked it up quickly. When I tried to eat it again, a British woman spoke to me. She said, “5-second rule, it’s okay!” and smiled at me. I was surprised to hear that the 5-second rule doesn’t have borders!
