My daughter has a six-year old Shiba Inu named Chachamaru. I love dogs, and when I don’t have to work, I take him for a walk. My daughter and her family live in my neighborhood, so I can walk him anytime. I usually send the family a message on LINE the night before and ask if it’s okay. On Tuesday night, my granddaughter answered that I could walk him on Wednesday morning. When I opened the door at 7:30 a.m. yesterday, Chacha was glad to see me. I let him go where he wanted to go, and we came back 40 minutes later. I found out last night that my granddaughter had forgotten my message. She had walked Chacha before she went to school. Two walks in one morning. What a lucky dog!
