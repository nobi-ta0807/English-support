My wife and I flew from Japan to Munich, Germany, at the beginning of September, and we traveled by train and bus to Austria and Czechia. Then we went back to Munich and caught our return flight on September 14. We were glad to get back to Japan, but I was a little disappointed because I missed the biggest beer festival in the world, the Oktoberfest. It began on September 16 this year, just two days after we left! Munich is the home of six great breweries (beer makers). They put up tents and serve their beer in one-liter mugs along with roast chicken, sausages, and pretzels. The festival isn’t only for beer drinkers. There are lots of rides and activities for children, too.
