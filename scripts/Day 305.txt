Going to the World Series!
Yesterday, the TV sports announcer began with some big news about Ohtani Shohei. His dream is coming true. He is going to the World Series for the first time! Although he has been one of the best players in Major League Baseball for several years, his team wasn’t very strong. Then, this year, he moved to the Dodgers, and his new team won more games than any other team in the National League. In the playoffs, the Dodgers beat the San Diego Padres first, and now they have beaten the New York Mets. They are the National League champions. They will play the New York Yankees, the American League champions, in the World Series. I’m rooting for the Dodgers!
