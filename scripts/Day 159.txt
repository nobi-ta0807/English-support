I have three enemies. The first one is snacks. They are very light but powerful. The power that makes snacks delicious is their calories. By the time I realize it, they are already in my stomach. The second is games. I can't stop when I start playing a game. But I have a strong ally in this situation. The ally is my father. He scolds me for playing games for a long time. When he scolds me for just starting the game, however, he also becomes an enemy. The third is my bed. It's the most frightening of the three. It becomes stronger in winter. It won't let go of me in the morning. I always lose this battle. However, I will never give up.
