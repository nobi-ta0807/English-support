During this winter vacation, I went on a trip to Nara with my classmates. We had very little money, so we stayed in the cheapest hotel possible. The bath in that hotel was so small that we couldn't get in, so we decided to go to a public bath at night. It was built in the 1960s, and the building has not changed for about 60 years. It was very retro and cool. When we entered the bathtub, there were only people with tattoos in it. They looked scary, and they made me very nervous. Then I listened to what they were talking about. They were discussing whether cats or dogs were cuter. That made me relax!
