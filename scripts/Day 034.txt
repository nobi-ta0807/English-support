July 4 is one of the most important public holidays in the U.S.A. It is called Independence Day because American leaders signed the Declaration of Independence on this day in 1776. The Americans had to fight Britain for several years after that, but when they won, a new country was born. Nowadays Americans celebrate the holiday with the biggest firework displays of the year. There are parades and concerts in cities all over the country, and Boston has the biggest celebrations of all. This is because Boston was where the war for independence started. This year's events began on June 30 and will last through July 4. You can see big ships from 200 years ago in the waters around the city, and there is music and food everywhere.
