Camellias are winter flowers, and you can see them all over Japan. They have been blooming in Nagoya for more than one month, and they are particularly beautiful this year. I think that the mild winter is the reason why there are so many flowers on each plant. The camellias near my house are mostly dark pink, but there are white and red camillias, too. The leaves of the camellia plant are a dark green, like the leaves of the tea plant. That’s not surprising. The tea plant is actually one kind of camellia. It has the scientific name camellia sinensis.
