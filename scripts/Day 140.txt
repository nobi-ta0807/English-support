Two famous Japanese pitchers are going to the Major Leagues this year. The first to leave Japan was Yamamoto Yoshinobu of the Orix Buffaloes. He chose the Los Angeles Dodgers, and now that team has two great Japanese players, Yamamoto and Ohtani Shohei. I’m sure that a lot of fans will be flying from Japan to see Dodgers games during the 2024 season. Then, at the end of last week, Imanaga Shota of the DeNA BayStars signed with the Chicago Cubs. He is well known outside Japan because of his pitching in the 2019 and the 2023 World Baseball Classic championships. I’m happy for both of them, but I don't want all the great players to leave! I want to see some of them stay and play in NPB.
