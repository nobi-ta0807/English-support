The Saturn Eclipse
The moon covered Saturn on the night of December 8. This is called the Saturn Eclipse. Did you see it? I did. Saturn is one of the brightest planets in the sky, so I thought it would be easy to see. But the moon was so bright that it was a little hard to see Saturn. Unlike a solar or a lunar eclipse, it’s hard to see what happens when Saturn is hidden. The best thing is to watch when Saturn disappears and then comes back. Luckily, the weather was clear, so I could see it clearly. It was extremely cold, though.
