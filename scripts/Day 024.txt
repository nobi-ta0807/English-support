There is a website called “Nagoya is not Boring.” It was started in 2020 by two women, one Spanish and the other German. They love Nagoya, but they were unhappy with Nagoya’s boring reputation. They wanted to show that Nagoya was really interesting, so they created this website for foreign tourists. They do small group tours in Spanish, German, and English and take people around Nagoya and to nearby places. There are walking tours of the old parts of Nagoya, eating and drinking tours, and many hands-on experiences such as learning ikebana and local cooking. They will even take you to the Nagoya Grand Sumo tournament! If you still think Nagoya is boring, take a look at their site!
