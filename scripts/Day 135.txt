Las Vegas is a popular place for Americans to get married, and many couples do it on New Year’s Eve (December 31). Last year, the number was three or four times higher than usual. Why? Because Americans write the date in a different way. Here in Japan, the day comes first, then the month, and finally the year, so December 31, 2023, becomes 311223. That’s not such an interesting number. In the U.S., people write the month first, then the day, and finally the year. Last New Year’s Eve was 123123. That’s a very interesting number, so more people than usual decided to get married on that date.
