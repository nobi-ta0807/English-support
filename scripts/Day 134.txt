Where were you on January 1 at 16:10? That was when a 7.6-magnitude earthquake (7 on the Japanese scale) hit Ishikawa Prefecture. It was so strong that people all over Honshu could feel it. Fires destroyed a large area of Wajima, and many buildings have fallen down in Ishikawa and neighboring prefectures. People have lost their homes and are living in shelters. It’s difficult to get food and water to the area because many roads are damaged. And the earthquakes aren't over. There have been smaller earthquakes every day since January 1. It will take a years before life in Ishikawa is normal again. If you want to help, don't go to Ishikawa right now. Give money or things that people need.
