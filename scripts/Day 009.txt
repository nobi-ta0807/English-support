I remember seeing rental electric scooters for the first time when I went back to the U.S. in 2019. People used a special smartphone app to rent them. Tourists were riding them everywhere around Washington, D.C. Now, an e-scooter rental service has started in Nagoya. Other big cities such as Tokyo and Kyoto already have the service. Like the rental service in the U.S., you use an app to rent one. You can find the nearest rental e-scooters on your smartphone. You pick up the e-scooter at a special location, and you can return it to the same or a different location. Right now, you need a driver’s license to ride an e-scooter, but that law is going to change. In July, anyone 16 years old and over will be able to ride an e-scooter without a license.
