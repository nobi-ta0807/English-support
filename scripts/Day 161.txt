Japan’s newest rocket lifted off from the Tanegashima Space Center last Saturday. Last year, when they tried to send up the H3 rocket for the first time, there was a problem, but this time, the H3 liftoff was a success. Japan plans to use this rocket for government and commercial missions from now on.  When I saw the liftoff on TV, it reminded me of liftoffs at the Kennedy Space Center in Florida. There were a lot of rocket fans watching and cheering as the rocket went up, and the rocket scientists were clapping and cheering, too. However, there was something very Japanese about the scene. Some of the scientists were crying tears of joy.
