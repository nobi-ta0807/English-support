A 65-year-old man who was born in Kobe now lives in Sapporo. Learning how to build log houses in Canada made him a professional builder, and he has worked all over Japan. He rides his motorcycle and goes overseas in his free time. He has been to 16 countries already. His policy is to make a hotel reservation only for the first night and go looking for places to stay after that. With a smile, he told me that seeing an aurora in Alaska or doing a triathlon race in Sadogashima were phenomenal. Now he is recharging his batteries in order to achieve his next dream: to stay in Scandinavia when he becomes 70 years old. This unique person is my condominium manager. He greets residents and cleans the entrance hall every day.
