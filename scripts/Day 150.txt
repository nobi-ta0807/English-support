On Saturday, I met some teachers who read Jaremaga. When we started talking about finding stories for Jaremaga, they said that I should write about Japanese slang, and they taught me a new word, “taipa.” I already knew “kosupa.” The “kosupa” or “cost performance” is good at a restaurant where the food is good and the prices are low. “Taipa” stands for “time performance.” Here is an example. When I listen to a podcast, I play it at 1.25 times the regular speed. I can still understand what the speaker is saying, but it takes less time to finish the podcast, so the “taipa” is good. By the way, these are Japanese words. We don't use "cost performance" or "time performance" in English.
