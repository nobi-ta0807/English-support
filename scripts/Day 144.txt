How do you know whether countries around the world are cheaper or more expensive than your own country? You can compare prices of things, but that can be difficult because each country sells many things. Besides, most countries use different currencies (money). A British magazine, the Economist, created the Big Mac Index in 1986 to make it easier to compare countries. The index shows the price of a Big Mac in almost 80 different countries. All the prices are in U.S. dollars, so it is easy to see which countries are cheap and which are expensive. Actually, Japan is cheap. A Big Mac here costs $2.83, but in the U.S., it costs $5.15!
