It’s summer, and people have more free time than usual. How about spending your time reading a book? Unlike TV and movies, books don’t start and finish at a certain time. The time it takes to read a book depends on how fast you read, what kind of book you are reading, and if you are enjoying it. My daughter lent me a mystery by Higashino Keigo last week. It took me almost a week to read the first half of the book, but the more exciting the story became, the faster I read. It only took me two days to finish the second half. I didn’t check my email or go on the Internet, and I hardly talked to my wife. I was in another world.
