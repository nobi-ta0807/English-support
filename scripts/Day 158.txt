Today is Valentine’s Day, and it is chocolate time! One department store at Nagoya Station is having a special chocolate fair called “Amour de chocolat.” If you want to go, today is the last day. The name is French and means “love of chocolate.” You can find 150 different chocolate brands from all over the world. You can also meet some of the chocolatiers who make the chocolates. I went there last week, but there were so many people and the lines were so long that I decided not to buy anything. Almost all the shoppers were women. I wonder if they give these special chocolates to their boyfriends. A friend said that they buy most of the chocolate for themselves.
