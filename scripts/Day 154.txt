The Grammy Awards ceremony was held on February 4, and the big winners were all women this year. The best album was “Midnights” by Taylor Swift. Swift made history. She is the first person to win a Grammy for best album four times. Billie Eilish won the best song of the year for “What Was I Made for?” and Miley Cyrus won the record of the year for “Flowers.” Victoria Monet won the award for best new artist.
