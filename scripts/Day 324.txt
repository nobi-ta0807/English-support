Robert is Here
A long time ago, I wrote about a six-year-old boy who sold cucumbers for his father at a roadside stand. Robert’s father made a sign that said, “Robert is Here,” and business was great. Robert made money and bought some land at the age of 14. He grew fruit and made his fruit and vegetable stand bigger. I was excited to see the Robert is Here Fruit Stand on my way to Everglades National Park. Robert is now 67 years old, and his stand has become famous. You can buy many different types of tropical fruit and vegetables there. They make milkshakes and smoothies with the fruit, so I ordered a passion fruit milkshake. It was delicious!
