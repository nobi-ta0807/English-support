Today is probably the coldest day of the year in Nagoya. It is -1 degree, and the weather report says that there is a 50% chance of snow. I have to go to work this morning, but should I drive or take a subway and train? I have all-weather tires on my car, and they say that this kind of tire is good on snow. However, the road might be very slippery. I’m afraid of the other drivers. If they brake suddenly, their car might skid and hit my car, or there might be traffic accidents along the way. After thinking about it, I have decided not to drive. I will take the subway and train. It takes longer, but I can relax and stay safe.
