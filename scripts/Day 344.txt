Here Comes Santa Claus
Have you seen Santa Claus this year? He is everywhere. You can see him in many different places. For example, he was at a big department store in Nagoya last weekend, and they say that he will be back again next weekend. Actually, he was at my house yesterday with a big bag of presents, and he gave me one, but he told me not to open it until Christmas Day. When he left, he went to visit some children in my neighborhood. He had presents for them, too. I hope he comes to your house this winter and brings all the presents that you want!
