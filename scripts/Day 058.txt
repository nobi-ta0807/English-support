Japan’s oldest newspaper is the “Mainichi Shinbun.” It goes back to 1872 (Meiji 5) and celebrated its 150th anniversary last year. The oldest newspaper in the world is probably the Wiener Zeitung in Austria. It goes back 320 years to 1703. It has a lot of history. For example, in 1768, there was an article about a talented 12-year-old musician. I think you know his name: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart. However, the Wiener Zeitung stopped selling a paper edition at the end of June this year. In the last paper edition, there was an interview with Arnold Schwarzenegger, the actor who was born and grew up in Austria. Now people can only read this newspaper online.
