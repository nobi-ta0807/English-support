At the beginning of the year, many people promise to do something to make themselves better. This is called a New Year’s resolution. According to Forbes, an American business magazine, the top resolution for 2024 is to become fitter (stronger and healthier). The second most popular resolution is to save money. Giving up cigarettes used to be one of the top resolutions, but it is only number seven this year. I think that’s because there are fewer smokers these days. They say that you have a better chance of keeping your resolution if you promise to do something. It’s more difficult if you try to stop doing something like smoking or drinking alcohol. What is your New Year's resolution for 2024?
