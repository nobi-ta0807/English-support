Doctor Yellow is in Nagoya
Doctor Yellow is in Nagoya now. I know a junior high school boy who is crazy about trains, and he told me a lot about this special yellow train. It is used to check the tracks and the overhead wires of the Shinkansen. It went from Tokyo to Hakata Station three times a month, but JR never told people the schedule. However, this boy knew it. Last year, he told me when it would come to Nagoya, but I was too busy to go to Nagoya Station to see it. He was disappointed when JR Tokai stopped running Doctor Yellow in January this year, but I think he is happy now. He can see it whenever he wants to at the SCMAGLEV and Railway Park, a train museum at the Kinjo Pier in Nagoya.
