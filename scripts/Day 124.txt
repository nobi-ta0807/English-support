This year is coming to an end, and the highlight of the year was going abroad for the first time since the pandemic was over. My husband and I went to Oahu in October. The purpose of this trip was to find the grave of a Japanese American. His father was an immigrant, and during World War II, four sons fought separately, two in the Japanese army and two in the American army. Thanks to the Internet and the help of some kind Americans, we were able to find where the grave was and visit it. At the same time, we learned what a hard time immigrants had. We also enjoyed some beautiful scenery and delicious food. This trip was meaningful and made a deep impression on us.
