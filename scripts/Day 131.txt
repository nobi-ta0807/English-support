A group of junior high school students came from Korea to visit Fujimae Tidal Flat over the weekend. They and their chaperones (adults who came with them) were able to stay until December 25 because Christmas is a holiday in Korea. Some children from our group in Japan spent two days with the Korean children. How did they communicate? They played games, did activities, and used English. There was one Korean girl who spoke Japanese. She learned it by herself by listening to J-pop. It’s amazing what children can do when they really want to.
