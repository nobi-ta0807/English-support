David Kirke was a writer and editor, but it was one of his pranks that made him famous. Kirke was a student at Oxford University when he and his friends started the “Dangerous Sports Club.” They jumped off a bridge in Bristol, England in 1979 using a special elastic rope made for the military. Kirke jumped first. The group was arrested by the police, but they were quickly released after paying a small fine (money). When New Zealander A.J. Hacket found out about the jump, he decided to start a bungee jumping business. Despite his dangerous pranks, Kirke lived to the age of 78.
