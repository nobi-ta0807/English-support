I went with a group of children to South Korea over the weekend. We flew from Nagoya to Busan on Friday. I was lucky because I had a window seat. I love to look at maps, and when I fly, I try to find landmarks that show me where I am. I was sitting next to a high school student, so I pointed out the Suzuka Mountains. Then we flew across Lake Biwa. We were sitting on the left side of the plane, and we could see Okishima Island, part of Omi Hachiman. We could also see the southern end of Lake Biwa and Biwako Bridge. It looked just like a map!
