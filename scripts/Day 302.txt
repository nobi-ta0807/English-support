Look up at the Sky!
Have you ever seen a comet? If you look up at the western sky just after the sun sets, you should be able to see one. It looks like a star with a tail. This comet is called Tsuchinshan-ATLAS. Comets come back regularly, but scientists say that this comet will not come back for another 80,000 years. Now is your only chance to see it! People are posting photos of it all over the Internet. According to the news last night, you should be able to see it until the end of this week. You can see it with the naked eye, but it’s better if you use binoculars or a telescope.
