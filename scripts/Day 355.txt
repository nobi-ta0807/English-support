A Banana Problem
When should you eat a banana? When it is a little green, when it is all yellow, or after it starts to get brown? That depends on the person. I heard about a couple who had a banana problem. The husband kept eating all the bananas. His wife never ate any. Finally, he asked her why she bought bananas if she didn’t like them. She said that he ate all the bananas before they were ripe. He liked bananas that were still a little green, but she wanted the bananas to get a little brown so that she was sure they were sweet. Now they understand each other, and he leaves some bananas to get ripe for his wife.
