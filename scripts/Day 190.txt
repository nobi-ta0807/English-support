Have you ever met someone that you knew in an unexpected place? If you have, you can say, “It’s a small world!” That happened to me yesterday. I was working at one of the Fujimae Tidal Flat centers near Nagoya Port yesterday when someone suddenly said, “Jarrell-sensei!” It was an old student of mine. After graduating from university six years ago, she became an elementary school teacher. She came to the center because she is planning to bring her students there in a couple of months, and she wanted to look around first. We only talked for a few minutes, but it was great to see that she is doing well as a teacher.
