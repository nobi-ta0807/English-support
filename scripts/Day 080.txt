The other day I came across the acronym "ASMR" in a newspaper. It was new to me. The article about a 10-year-old boy who made his debut as a kabuki actor. He likes making beat and whisper ASMR videos when he is free. When I checked the meaning of ASMR, I realized that I've often seen ASMR on TV or on the web. One of the ASMR I like at the moment is the “good wake-up sound” (“mezame no iine”) that I watch every day. I asked the junior high school daughter of a friend whether or not she knew about ASMR. Her answer was, “Yes!” She likes the sound of someone eating something crispy like potato chips and often watches this kind of video. It reminds me of sound healing CDs that were popular many years ago. What kind of ASMR do you enjoy? Or is ASMR new to you?
