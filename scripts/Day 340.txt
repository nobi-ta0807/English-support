Be Careful Tomorrow!
Be careful tomorrow! It’s Friday the 13th. You probably know that it is an unlucky day, but do you know what you shouldn’t do on this day? Here is a list. 1. Don’t break a mirror. It will bring you seven years of bad luck. 2. Don’t walk under a ladder. Something, or someone, might fall on you. 3. Don’t spill salt, but if you do, throw some over your left shoulder. 4. Don’t let a black cat cross in front of you. It is a sign of bad luck. 5. Don’t travel or get surgery on Friday the 13th. Try to do these things on another day.
