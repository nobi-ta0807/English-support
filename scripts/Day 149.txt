Is Aichi Prefecture the new center of tennis for men in Japan? It looks like that. Last Saturday, two 17-year-olds from the prefecture that I live in became champions at the Australian Open. Oda Tokito won the wheelchair men’s singles title, beating his rival, Alfie Hewett of Britain. Sakamoto Rei won the boys’ singles title, beating Jan Kumstat of the Czech Republic. I heard about Oda Tokito last year when he won the wheelchair men’s singles title at the French Open and then at Wimbleton, but Sakamoto Rei was new to me. Go Aichi power!
