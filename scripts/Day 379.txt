I’m Glad I Did!
If you want to borrow a paper book, you have to go to the library. These days, however, you can borrow e-books from your own home. I went online and borrowed an e-book in January. I chose “Demon Copperhead,” a story about a boy who has a very hard life growing up in the countryside. It was moving and full of surprises, but I’m a slow reader. I was only halfway through the book the day before it was due. If it were a paper book, I could finish it and take it back late, but this was an e-book. I had to finish it on time! I stayed up until 3 a.m. the night before and I finally finished it! I’m glad I did! When I checked my tablet the next day, the book had disappeared.
