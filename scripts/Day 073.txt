What do you imagine when you think of meals? In Japan, I have three meals a day: a hot breakfast early in the morning, a simple sandwich and salad around noon, and a hot dinner in the early evening. In Austria, I have been staying with a family on vacation. Life is slow, and we don’t have breakfast until 9:00 a.m. There is no hot food, but it’s delicious: four or five types of fresh bread straight from the bakery, eight types of cheese and a variety of sausage and ham, specialties of Austria. They also serve a variety of homemade jams. We have a late lunch, sometime between 3:00 and 5:00 p.m. This is the only hot meal of the day. It includes soup with bread and a delicious meat dish or pasta. We finish the day with a light meal in the evening, usually bread, cheese, and sausage again. I love bread, cheese, sausage, and soup, so it’s the perfect menu for me!
