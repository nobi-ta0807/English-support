I was driving back home from work on Friday when I stopped at a traffic light. I noticed something orange on the back bumper of the car in front of me. It was a butterfly! The light turned green, and the car started to move. The butterfly folded its wings and hung on to the car. The car was traveling in the direction of my house, so I followed it, I kept my eye on the bumper. When the car stopped at the next red light, the butterfly was still there. I followed it for about two kilometers. Then the car turned right. I wanted to keep following it, but I decided to go straight home. How much longer did it stay on the bumper? Was it enjoying its free ride?
