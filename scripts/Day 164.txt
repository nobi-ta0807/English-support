Someone spoke to me in English near the station. "Could you tell me how to go to the International Congress Center?" I thought for about 10 seconds and then said, "OK. Go straight and turn left at the second corner. Then go straight along the street for about three minutes, and you can see it on your left." He thanked me and left. Three days later, I met him at the same place again. He said, "Thanks to you I could make it to the meeting on time. Japanese students are wonderful." I felt proud and decided to study English harder in order to explain more clearly the next time someone speaks to me in English.
