I'm what you call a “rain bringer.” Once, my textbook got wet due to heavy rain, and another time, a typhoon hit in the middle of my trip. One day in July, I was going home from school with my friend as usual. Perhaps because it was a hot day, it suddenly started raining. My friend said, “Oh, my God! I don't have an umbrella!” Do you think my friend got wet because I was a rain bringer? Don't worry! As I am a rain bringer, I always carry two folding umbrellas! I was careful, so we didn't get wet. The truth is that I wanted a chance to say, “Please use my umbrella.”
