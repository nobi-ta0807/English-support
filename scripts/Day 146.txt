Do you know where the oldest hotel in the world is? According to the Guinness book, it is in Japan. The Nishiyama Hot Spring, Keiunkan in Yamanashi Prefecture was founded (started) about 1,300 years ago. Of course, the buildings have changed, but the same family still runs the inn. They say that Takeda Shingen and Tokugawa Ieyasu were visitors to the hot spring. These days anyone can stay at the inn. It is a little expensive, but it has a beautiful traditional Japanese design. Besides, it is a great place to relax because of its indoor and outdoor baths.
