I cycled 30 km to see an old friend. He and I are in our 70s, but we hadn’t seen each other for 40 years, so I was actually not sure whether he was alive or not. When I arrived at his home, I saw someone who didn't have the face or body of the younger man I used to know. He also didn't seem to know who I was. He was very surprised after hearing my name. We were full of nostalgia. We chatted a lot for two hours. I had to ride back 30 km on my bike, so we promised to see each other again, and I headed home.
