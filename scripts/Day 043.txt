Carlos Alcatraz won the Wimbleton men’s singles by beating 36-year-old Novak Djokovic last weekend. Alcatraz is one of the youngest winners ever, only 20 years old. The crowd included stars like Brad Pitt and Prince William and his family.  Oda Tokito may not have had so many stars watching him, but he won the Wimbleton men’s wheelchair singles at the age of 17. He was playing Alfie Hewett of Great Britain, and just like at the French Open one month ago, he beat Hewett in two straight sets. He is Wimbleton's youngest winner ever. As Oda said after the match, “I’m still only 17, so I want to open the champagne, but I can’t. I have to drink sparkling water.”
