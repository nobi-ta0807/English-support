We Loved It!
American movies with a Christmas theme come out at the end of each year. A couple of weeks ago, I heard about an interesting movie from 2023. It was at a movie theater in Nagoya, so I took my wife to see it. “The Holdovers” takes place at a boys’ boarding school in the snowy northeast of the U.S. Most of the boys go home for Christmas, but a few unlucky ones have to stay at the school. The teacher who looks after them is very strict. Nobody is happy at first, but they change as they get to know each other. The movie is very funny and sometimes sad, and the acting is great. My wife and I don’t always agree, but both of us loved this movie!
