I went to Tokyo to give a presentation over the weekend. There wasn’t a cloud in the sky as I passed Mt. Fuji on the Shinkansen. I always look forward to seeing Japan’s most beautiful mountain, especially in winter, and this time I got a nice shot (photo) of the mountain with snow on top. Before my presentation, the teacher who invited me to Tokyo took me to a local hot spring. We washed ourselves in regular water, but then we went into an outdoor bath. The water was black! It felt great, but the water was so black that I couldn’t see more than a few centimeters below the surface. This kind of black-water hot spring is common in Kanto.
