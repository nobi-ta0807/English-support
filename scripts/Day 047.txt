It was 30 years ago today that one of the most exciting computer graphic films ever made came out. “Jurassic Park” was released on July 24, 1993. The story came from the bestselling book of the same name. The director was Steven Spielberg, the man who had made one of my favorite movies, “Jaws.” There were no computer graphics in “Jaws,” but this time, Spielberg decided to create a world of dinosaurs with CGI. I knew that this was a movie I had to see in a movie theater. I went with my wife. The dinosaurs looked so real and the action scenes were so exciting that the movie kept us on the edge of our seats.
