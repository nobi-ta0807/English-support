Yesterday was Coming of Age Day, but how old are people in Japan when they come of age? They used to become adults at the age of 20. Then, a few years ago, the law changed. Now 18-year-olds can vote in elections. They can borrow money, rent apartments, and get credit cards without the help of their parents. However, there are still some things that they cannot do. They are not allowed to drink or smoke. So when should they celebrate Coming of Age Day? I thought that most people would go to celebratiions when they were 18, but that isn't true. When I asked some first-year university students, I was surprised that no one was going to celebrate Coming of Age Day this year. They were all going to wait until they were 20.
