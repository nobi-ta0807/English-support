Tomorrow is an important holiday in France. In English, we call it Bastille Day, but it is usually just called July 14 in French. In 1789, the people of Paris were angry at the King because he had all the power. Bread was very expensive and people were hungry, but he didn’t help them. The Bastille was a prison inside Paris and a symbol of the King. The people attacked the prison and let out the prisoners. Then, later that year, they destroyed the Bastille. The attack on July 14 was the start of the French Revolution. Nowadays, French people celebrate this day with fireworks like in the U.S., and they hold a big military parade on the Champs-Elysees. Fighter planes fly over making the red, white and blue French flag in the sky.
