A Google Street View Car
I passed a Google Street View car the other day while I was driving. I saw the same car again yesterday. It was a small silver car, and it said “Street View on Google Maps” on the side. It was going around my neighborhood while the weather was good. This car has 15 cameras on top, and it takes very clear photos. You can use Google Street View to see what a place looks like before you go there. Google updates Street View once every 1-3 years. I wonder if my car will be in the next update. Actually, I know someone who found his own photo on Street View. He was standing on a street in Hong Kong when a Google car came by. He held up his smartphone and took a photo of the car. When he checked Street View later, he was in the picture!
