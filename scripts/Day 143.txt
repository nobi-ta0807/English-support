Inoue Naoya is probably the best boxer in the world now. He has won all 26 of his professional fights since he entered professional boxing in 2012, and last year he became the world champion in four divisions. He is called “monster” because of his power. He has won almost every fight with a knock-out. Last Friday, the Boxing Writers Association of America gave him the Sugar Ray Robinson Award for the “Fighter of the Year.” You know how good Inoue is when you look at the names of the other boxers who have gotten this award: Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson, and Manny Pacquiao.
