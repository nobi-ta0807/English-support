An Unbelievable Happening
My family always watches the morning news. One morning, I thought we were watching the news as usual. I looked at the time on the TV and thought it was still early, so I got ready slowly. I don’t usually look at my smartphone in the morning, but I decided to check it this time. When I picked it up, I noticed something strange—the time on my phone was very different from the time on the TV. Confused, I asked my mom about it. She replied, “Oh? This is a recorded program.” I looked closer and saw the date on the TV. It was from last week. Of course, I was late for school.
