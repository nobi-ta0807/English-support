The Nagoya Grand Sumo Tournament is on now, and you can go to the different stables in the Nagoya area and watch the wrestlers training in the morning. I went to the Isegahama stable on Sunday. The wrestlers practiced pushing each other, and the high-ranking wrestlers gave the low-ranking wrestlers advice. I couldn’t help laughing when I saw one of the smaller wrestlers pick up an insect (probably a cicada) and walk up to a much bigger wrestler. The bigger wrestler was scared and ran away! Then the smaller wrestler threw the insect at him. He jumped back! I thought, “Boys will be boys!” After the training session, the wrestlers posed and let everyone take photos. The wrestlers may act like children sometimes, but they look like very powerful adult men when you stand next to them.
