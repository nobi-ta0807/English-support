Have you ever seen “America’s Got Talent”? It is a very popular TV talent show, and people can do any kind of performance they want to. This year, there were 55 acts which included singing, dancing, magic, comedy, and animal acts. Two Japanese dance teams were so good that they made it to the finals. One was Chibi Unity, a dance team from Niigata. This team has been winning dance contests in Japan since 2017. The other was Avantgardey, a dance team that started last year. They give unique and comical performances. Go online and watch the performances of both dance teams. You will be impressed!
