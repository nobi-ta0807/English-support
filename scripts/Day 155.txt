On New Year's Day, my family and I had "osechi" (special New Year's food) together. We ate many foods that we usually eat only at the New Year such as "kuromame," "kazunoko," "kurikinton," "datemaki," and "kamaboko." In my family, crabs are served in addition to "osechi." Every year, they disappear from the table quickly. This year, only the claw of one leg was left. My brother suggested a game of rock-paper-scissors to decide who would win it. To my surprise, he won. He guessed that everyone else would choose "scissors." We were too caught up with the crab in front of us and fell for his strategy.
