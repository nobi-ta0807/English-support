Simple Gloves
One day, while shopping with my mother, I saw some cute red gloves on a mannequin. They were made of yarn and had a string to hang around the neck. I asked if we could buy them, but my mother said no. Instead, she suggested that my grandmother could make similar ones. When we got home, I messaged my grandmother. A few days later, a pair of adorable pink gloves arrived. They were handmade and looked just like the ones in the store. Now, I wear them to school every day. They may not be fancy, but I love them because my grandmother made them just for me!
