When I was a child, I went on a trip with my family every summer. I was looking forward to leaving for the destination, but my father always began to exercise and read a book in the morning. We had to wait for him until he finished everything. Then, we climbed a mountain and went to see a lake. It was boring for me at that time. But now I wish I could have the same experiences with him again and talk to him about the trips. When he left us, goodbyes came suddenly. I want to talk to him again and tell him, "Thank you for the good memories, Dad! I will always love you!"
