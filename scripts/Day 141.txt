My family and I were looking at old photos last night. When my daughter saw a photo of me as a boy, she was surprised that my hair was blond. By the time she was born, my hair had turned brown. Actually, this is natural among many children with blond hair. Their hair is very light when they are born, but it becomes darker as they grow older. Japanese children are the other way around. When they are young, they have black hair, but when they grow up, it suddenly becomes lighter. Of course, their hair color doesn’t change naturally. When students leave high school, they have more freedom, and many of them decide to dye their hair.
