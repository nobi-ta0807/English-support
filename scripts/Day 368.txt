Cat on a Quiet Night
I love looking at the moon and stars at night. One evening, while I was alone in the garden, I found a cute cat nearby. Since I love cats, I tried to touch it. The friendly cat let me stroke it for a long time. The quiet night created a special world for us. After that, I became friends with other cats too, and I looked at the sky with them almost every night. I cherished those moments, but soon they were gone. Sometimes, I see the cats during the day, and I can’t forget those nights.
