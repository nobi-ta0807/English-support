On January 5, an Alaska Airlines airplane left Portland, Oregon for a city in California. The plane was about 4,500 meters above the ground when one of the doors suddenly came off. There was a big hole where the door used to be. Air rushed out of the plane, but all the passengers were safe because they were wearing seat belts. However, some of their things were pulled out of the plane. A man found a cell phone on the ground later. It wasn’t locked, so he opened it. He found information about Alaskan Airlines flight 1282. The phone had fallen out of the sky, and it was still working! It probably didn't break because it was flat and floated down, and it fell into trees and onto soft grass.
