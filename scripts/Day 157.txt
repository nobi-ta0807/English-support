Seiji Ozawa has passed away at the age of 88. He was one of the most famous conductors in the world. He won several prizes for conducting in Europe in his 20s. Then he worked with Leonard Bernstein, composer of “West Side Story” and conductor of the New York Philharmonic Orchestra. He went on to become famous as the conductor of the Boston Symphony Orchestra where he stayed for 29 years, longer than any other conductor. He was known for playing modern pieces as well as famous classical pieces. In Boston, he worked with John Williams, the composer of music for many famous movies like “Jaws” and “Stars Wars.” He was truly a superstar in the world of classical music.
