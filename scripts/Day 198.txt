I stream a lot of movies at home, but I really like to go to a movie theater. I love the smell of popcorn. I always buy some and a nice, cold soft drink. I enjoy sitting in the movie theater and waiting in suspense for the movie to begin. Last Thursday, I decided to go see “Dune, Part 2” with my son-in-law. We had already seen Part 1 when it came out, and we were impressed with the special effects. Dune is a planet of sand, but people fight over it because it has a very valuable product called spice. Giant worms live in the sand and make Dune a very dangerous place to live and work. The special effects are great, especially the giant sand worms.
