Yellow Dust from Mongolia
Spring is a wonderful season, but it has some bad points, too. One of them in the yellow dust that comes from Mongolia. The wind lifts the dust high up into the air from the desert and carries it east. The TV weather map shows a big cloud of dust moving toward Japan from China. The meteorologist, the person who reports the weather, says that it is bad for our health. We should stay inside as much as possible and wear a mask when we go outside. We should dry our clothes inside rather than outside so that they don’t get dust on them. I haven’t seen any dust, but I can see that the air isn’t clear. Although it’s sunny, the sky isn’t blue. Late in the afternoon, the dust turns the sun orange.
