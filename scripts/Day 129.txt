When I borrowed a book from the library, I found a slip of paper in the book. It was the previous borrower’s booklist. That person had borrowed four books of all different genres. All those titles grabbed my attention. There was an English novel, a Japanese novel, a travel book, and a health-related book. I thought to myself, “Wow, this person and I have similar tastes. I want to talk with her.“ Then I flipped over the paper and found a familiar handwritten note. It was my writing! The mysterious person was me two years ago. I totally forgot that I had borrowed the book before.
